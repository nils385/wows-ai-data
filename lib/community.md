# Community opinion: builds and meta

This page is **community opinion, not game data**. It summarizes what current (2026) build guides and player forums recommend, with the source of every point. Guides disagree and the meta changes with patches. Quote it as "players/guides recommend", not as fact. Check exact skill and upgrade effects on the ship pages ({BASE}/ship/{name}.md), which come from the game files.

Collected: 2026-11-18 (game version {VERSION}). Reddit could not be read directly; sources are build guides and community forums.

## Captain skills: common 21-point paths

Abbreviations: see {BASE}/glossary.md. A captain has 21 points; a skill costs its row number (1-4).

- **Destroyers**: Preventive Maintenance (PM) -> Last Stand (LS) -> Survivability Expert (SE) -> Concealment Expert (CE), then Radio Location (RL) on some lines, Superintendent (SI), Adrenaline Rush (AR) or Consumable Specialist. Concealment Expert is called near-mandatory (-10% detectability); LS and SE are treated as core for every destroyer. Sources: [warshipsgames.com builds 2026](https://warshipsgames.com/world-of-warships-best-builds/), [warshipsgames.com destroyer guide](https://warshipsgames.com/destroyers-world-warships-guide/)
- **Battleships**: PM -> AR -> SE -> CE, then SI, then Main Battery and AA Expert/Specialist (old names AFT/BFT), then Heavy AP Shells (HAP) or Focus Fire Training (FFT). Tank-style builds add Fire Prevention Expert. Sources: [warshipsgames.com builds 2026](https://warshipsgames.com/world-of-warships-best-builds/), [mmokb.com skills guide 2026](https://mmokb.com/world-of-warships-skills-guide/)
- **Cruisers**: Inertia Fuse for HE Shells (IFHE) is usually taken around the 17-point mark, mainly when a ship's HE cannot penetrate the plating it fights most (and often for tier VI-VII and Ranked). It is skipped when the HE already penetrates well, because it halves fire chance. Sources: [wows-recruitment.com cruiser builds 2026](https://www.wows-recruitment.com/2026/09/world-of-warships-cruisers-builds.html), [wowbuilds.com Worcester line](https://www.wowbuilds.com/guide/tech-tree-branches-analogous-premiums-and-t10-ships/cruisers/usn-cls-worcester-line), [warshipsgames.com builds 2026](https://warshipsgames.com/world-of-warships-best-builds/)
- Guides agree to follow a standard path for the first five skills and adapt the rest per ship. Source: [warshipsgames.com builds 2026](https://warshipsgames.com/world-of-warships-best-builds/)

Exact effect of each skill on a given ship: {BASE}/ships/{ship}/captain-skills.md (linked from each ship page).

## Upgrades

- Common battleship picks: Main Armaments Modification 1, Damage Control System Modification 1, Steering Gears or Damage Control Modification 2, Main Battery Modification 3 or Gun Fire Control System Modification 2. Older forum posts also suggest Secondary Battery Modification for secondary-focused ships. Sources: [Steam community discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/552990/discussions/7/1751273534860223045), [Steam: Bayern build](https://steamcommunity.com/app/552990/discussions/7/1642038749329319844)
- Wargaming's in-game recommended upgrades are called a safe default by players. Source: [Steam community discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/552990/discussions/7/1751273534860223045)
- Unique (legendary) upgrades: the Des Moines one is called "basically mandatory"; Yamato's suits long-range builds. Source: [warshipsgames.com builds 2026](https://warshipsgames.com/world-of-warships-best-builds/)

## What players say about tier X (2026)

- **Des Moines** is called the benchmark tier X cruiser. **Minotaur** and **Petropavlovsk** are also named as standouts (Petropavlovsk for durability). Sources: [wg-watch.com tier X meta 2026](https://wg-watch.com/en/article/best-tier-10-ships-2026-wows-meta), [levvvel.com](https://levvvel.com/world-of-warships/ships/)
- **Gearing** is called the all-rounder destroyer; **Marceau** is named as a standout French pick. Source: [wg-watch.com tier X meta 2026](https://wg-watch.com/en/article/best-tier-10-ships-2026-wows-meta)
- Battleship tier lists often name **Ohio**, **Schlieffen**, **Bourgogne** and **Vermont** near the top; Ohio for tank builds, Schlieffen for secondary builds, the Yamato line for long range. Sources: [warshipsgames.com battleship tier list 2026](https://warshipsgames.com/best-battleships-world-of-warships/), [warshipsgames.com builds 2026](https://warshipsgames.com/world-of-warships-best-builds/)
- Carriers: **Midway** is called the most recommended tier X carrier; **Audacious** is rated highly. Sources: [warshipsgames.com carrier tier list](https://warshipsgames.com/world-of-warships-aircraft-carrier-tier-list/), [wg-watch.com](https://wg-watch.com/en/article/best-tier-10-ships-2026-wows-meta)
- Tier lists disagree a lot and some are low quality. For performance, use player statistics sites (for example wows-numbers.com) rather than tier lists.

## What players say matters most

- Concealment is widely called the most important stat, especially for destroyers. Source: [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/552990/discussions/0/828204634466170971)
- Theoretical DPM overstates real damage when shells cannot penetrate the target; check penetration too. Source: [r/WoWsBlitz guide via pocketgamer.com](https://www.pocketgamer.com/world-of-warships-blitz/world-of-warships-blitz-tips-and-tricks-the-very-best-warships/)
- Radar ranges matter for destroyer play: a 7.5 km radar does not outrange most destroyer torpedoes. Source: [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/552990/discussions/0/4098792306724646191). Exact radar ranges: {BASE}/rankings/groups/consumables.md
